# Ontology Explorer

A business-glossary-centric visualization of the semantic context graph in OpenMetadata 1.13. It starts from business concepts (glossary terms) and maps how they relate to each other and to the data assets that implement them.

## Relationship to Knowledge Graph

- **Ontology Explorer** — Top-down: business concepts → implementing data assets
- **[[knowledge-graph]]** — Bottom-up: data assets → business meaning

Together they provide both directions of the same underlying [[unified-metadata-graph]]: from business meaning downward into data, and from data upward into business meaning.

## Version

Introduced in **OpenMetadata 1.13** (April 2026), alongside Knowledge Graph.

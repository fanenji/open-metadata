# OpenMetadata + DataOps + Agentic AI: Turning the Data Estate Into a Living City

Article by **Jeffrey Rodriguez Viaña** (May 2026) framing OpenMetadata, DataOps, and Agentic AI as three parts of a single operating model: **OpenMetadata is the map**, **DataOps is the traffic system**, and **Agentic AI is the dispatcher** that reads the map, watches the traffic, and helps people take action.

## Key Metaphors

- **Grocery store without labels**: Most enterprise data platforms are like a store where nothing has labels, expiration dates, or supplier information. OpenMetadata is the label system.
- **Hospital**: The data product is the patient, OpenMetadata is the medical chart, pipelines are nurses, data quality checks are vital signs, lineage is patient history.
- **Living city**: Data estate managed through the combination of metadata map + DataOps routines + AI dispatchers.

## Key Arguments

- **DataOps is operational discipline**, not just pipelines: treating data systems like production systems with freshness, correctness, ownership, documentation, and change management
- **Lineage is the family tree of data**: answers "Where did this come from?" — using query logs, view definitions, stored procedures, and SQL transformations
- **OpenMetadata unified knowledge graph** connects technical metadata, quality signals, lineage, ownership, usage, policies, conversations, glossaries, classifications, metrics, domains, and data products

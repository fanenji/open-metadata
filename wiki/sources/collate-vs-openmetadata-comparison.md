# Upgrading from OpenMetadata to Collate — The 3 Compelling Reasons

Vendor comparison page from **Collate** (the company behind OpenMetadata's managed service), positioning their managed offering against self-managed OpenMetadata.

## Three Arguments for Upgrading

1. **Advanced AI-powered and enterprise features** — AI-driven automation, generative AI data services, leadership dashboards, additional governance capabilities
2. **Expert-managed cloud hosting** — Eliminates DevOps, infrastructure, and staffing overhead required for self-managed OpenMetadata
3. **Production-ready support and SLAs** — Enterprise-grade support with guaranteed uptime and response times

## Key Claim

> "Running open source OpenMetadata requires DevOps, infrastructure, and staffing. Collate's managed OpenMetadata service not only frees teams to focus on their core responsibilities, but also provides additional capabilities designed for data and teams at scale."

> **Note**: This is vendor-authored promotional content. All claims should be evaluated against independent sources. The wiki's existing [[openmetadata]] coverage is based on the open-source product, independent of Collate's commercial offering.
